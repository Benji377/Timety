## Did you write about Timety?

I'd love to add it here! There are two easy ways:

1. **Open an issue** using the [“I wrote about Timety” form](../../issues/new?template=press-article.yml). Just fill in the link and a few details, and I'll add it to the list.
2. **Open a pull request** that adds a row to the table above, if you're comfortable with Git. Please keep the newest entries at the top.

### What fits here

- Articles, blog posts, reviews and tutorials
- Videos, podcasts and talks
- Social media posts or threads with real content (not just a one-line share)
- Any language is welcome

### What doesn't

- Auto-generated app-store listings or scraped directory pages without original content
- Pages that mostly host unrelated ads or malware-style download buttons

Entries link to the original source only: no excerpts or screenshots, so the traffic goes to the authors. 
If a link breaks or you'd like your entry changed or removed, just open an issue.
